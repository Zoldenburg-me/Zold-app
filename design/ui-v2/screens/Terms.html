Terms of use

The rules for using the Zold app. They sit alongside Monerium’s terms, which govern your IBAN and e-money.

Last updated [DATE]

1. What Zold is

Zold is software that lets you hold and move euros in a Safe smart account that you own. Zold is not a bank and is not a payment institution.

2. Who holds your money

Your euros are e-money (EURe) issued by Monerium ehf. They sit in your own smart account, never in an account of ours. Monerium’s terms apply to your IBAN, identity check and payouts.

3. Your passkey

The passkey on your device is the only owner of your account. Zold cannot sign payments for you, reverse them, or reset your passkey. Keep your device secure and set up recovery.

4. Payments

Before you approve a payment, the app shows the amount, the recipient, any fee and the arrival estimate. Your approval covers exactly that payment. If the recipient changes after approval, the payment does not go out.

5. Business accounts

A business account belongs to the company named when it was opened. The person who opens it confirms they may act for that company. Owners decide who can propose, approve and send payments.

6. What is not available

Some features are listed as not yet available, such as cash pickup and cards. They are not part of the service until the app offers them. See Regulatory notes.

7. Fees and plans

Zold charges no fee on SEPA payments. Paid plans cannot be bought yet; each account can start one 30-day trial. Ending a plan pauses paid features and deletes nothing.
