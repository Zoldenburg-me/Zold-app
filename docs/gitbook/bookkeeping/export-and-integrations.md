# Export and integrations

## CSV export

Premium and Business. Business dashboard → Transactions → **Export**.

The export contains every transaction in the current filter with date, wallet, asset, amount, counterparty, reporting-currency value, tags, chart-of-accounts mapping and, where a disposal occurred, the realised gain or loss. The monthly balance report exports the same way from **Reports**.

## Xero and QuickBooks

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Not yet fully live.** Direct sync to Xero and QuickBooks is rolling out to Business organisations. Until it reaches yours, the CSV export imports cleanly into both.
{% endhint %}

When enabled, Settings → **Integrations** connects your accounting ledger. Transactions flow into it mapped to the chart of accounts you keep in Zold, and each sync is logged with what was sent.
