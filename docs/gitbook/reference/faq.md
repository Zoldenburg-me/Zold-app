# FAQ

**Is Zold a bank?**
No. Zold is an app built by Zoldenburg. Your IBAN and your euros are issued and held as e-money by Monerium, a licensed e-money institution. Your balance sits in a smart wallet that only you can operate.

**Is my money insured?**
E-money is not covered by a deposit guarantee scheme. Instead, the issuer must safeguard the full value of every EURe in issue and you have a legal right to redeem at par. Read Monerium's terms for the detail.

**Can Zold freeze or move my money?**
Zold cannot move it: every payment is signed by you. Monerium, as the issuer, has the obligations of any regulated institution and may be required to act on your account by law.

**What happens if Zold shuts down?**
Your wallet and your EURe are on a public network and your passkey owns the wallet. Your redemption right is against Monerium, not Zold.

**Do I need to buy crypto or hold ETH?**
No. You use euros. Network fees are paid by Zold.

**Can I have more than one device?**
Yes, if your passkey syncs (iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager, a password manager). Each device also has its own device key for signing payments, created the first time you use the account there.

**I lost my phone.**
See [Security and recovery](../getting-started/security-and-recovery.md). If you enrolled email / SMS recovery, you can recover on a new device yourself. If not, contact support for managed recovery, which is slower and requires proving your identity again.

**Why was my quote refused?**
Usually because a fresh market rate could not be fetched, or because the venue price was too far from the market. Try again in a minute. Zold never quotes from an old rate.

**Why did my transfer go to Manual review?**
Something happened that should not be resolved automatically, such as a partner failing after it already held the funds. Your money is accounted for and someone will look at it. You will be contacted.

**Can I hold dollars?**
Soon. A US dollar account with its own account and routing number is being added through Bridge, and sits in the same wallet as your euros. See [Your US dollar account](../add-money/usd-account.md).

**Can I pay in a currency other than euros?**
Cash pickup in Kenya pays out shillings. Other currencies open as their accounts become available; see [Accounts and currencies](../business/accounts-and-currencies.md).

**Is my payment page private?**
No. See [Your payment page](../get-paid/payment-page.md).

**Does Zold give tax advice?**
No. The invoicing tools stop you from producing an invalid document under the rules Zold encodes, and say exactly what they do not check. Your accountant decides the rest.
