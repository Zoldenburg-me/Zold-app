# Welcome to Zold

Zold is **global accounts** for people and businesses whose money crosses borders.

A global account is a set of local accounts under one roof. Each one is denominated in the currency of a place, carries the identifier locals use, and pays out on that place's own rail: an IBAN in the euro area, an account and routing number in the United States. You hold them all in one smart wallet that only you can operate, and you move between them at a live, visible rate.

**Euros are open today.** You get a euro IBAN, and money that arrives by bank transfer is held as EURe, a regulated euro e-money token, in your wallet. From there you can pay any bank account in the SEPA area, or send cash that a relative collects at a MoneyGram counter.

**Dollars are next.** A US dollar account, with a US account number and routing number that receives ACH and wire and pays out to any US bank, is being added through Bridge. See [Your US dollar account](add-money/usd-account.md).

Businesses get everything a finance team needs on top: several people with different roles, payments that go through review before they move, invoices, an address book with bank details, and bookkeeping that exports to your accountant's software.

## How Zold is different

**One wallet, many accounts.** Your euros and your dollars sit in the same smart wallet on the Base network. Each currency has its own local identifier, but there is one balance sheet, one address book, one set of books.

**Your money stays yours.** The wallet's owner is a passkey on your device, and every payment is signed by you at the moment you send it. Zold cannot move your money without you, and cannot replace your keys.

**The money is regulated money.** EURe is issued by Monerium, an e-money institution licensed in the European Economic Area, backed one-for-one by euros with a redemption right at par. Dollars are held as USDC, issued by Circle and fully reserved. Your IBAN is a real IBAN; your US account number is a real account number.

**Prices are shown before you sign.** Every transfer and every conversion is quoted against a live mid-market rate. The fee and the measured margin are on the screen before you approve, and the rate you approve is the rate you get. If Zold cannot get a fresh rate, it refuses to quote rather than guess.
