# Crypto deposit

Open **Add money → Crypto wallet**. The screen shows your wallet address as text and as a QR code.

* **Network:** Base.
* **Token:** USDC.

Send USDC on Base to that address from any wallet or exchange. It arrives in your smart wallet directly; no one is in between.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Send only **USDC on the Base network**. Tokens sent on another network, or other tokens sent to this address, are not recognised by the app. They are not lost, since the wallet is yours, but Zold cannot see or spend them for you.
{% endhint %}

## Converting to euros

You can choose to have incoming USDC converted to EURe automatically. When the option is on, each deposit is priced against a live mid-market rate through the same liquidity venues Zold uses for payments, and the converted EURe is credited to your balance. The deposit row in Activity shows the amount received, the rate applied and the EURe credited.

A deposit that is too small to convert economically, or that arrives while the rate feed cannot be checked, is held as USDC and the reason is shown on the row.

## Paying you by link

If you want someone else to send you crypto without handing them a raw address, use your [payment page](../get-paid/payment-page.md).
