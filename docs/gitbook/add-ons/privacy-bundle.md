# Privacy Bundle

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Not yet fully live.** The Privacy Bundle appears in the app and can be reserved. Subscriptions start when provisioning with both partners is switched on, and nothing is charged before then.
{% endhint %}

The Privacy Bundle is Kokio eSIM data plus Mysterium VPN, sold as one monthly add-on for people who move between countries: mobile data that works when you land, and a private connection on hotel and airport Wi-Fi.

| Plan | Included eSIM data | Included VPN | Devices |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Travel Shield | 3 GB regional | 25 GB | 3 |
| Global Shield | 10 GB global | 100 GB | 5 |
| Nomad Shield | 25 GB global | 250 GB | 10 |

Allowances are per month and reset with the billing period. Top-ups will be available.

## What it is not

It is not unlimited, and it is not anonymous banking. Your Zold account and your identity with Monerium are unaffected by whether you use the bundle. Region availability for the eSIM is shown in the app at the moment you activate, not assumed.

Profile → **Privacy bundle**.
