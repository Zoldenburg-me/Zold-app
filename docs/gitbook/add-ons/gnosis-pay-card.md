# Gnosis Pay card

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Not yet fully live.** Today you can connect an existing Gnosis Pay account and see its cards, balances and transactions in Zold. Applying for a card and topping it up from your Zold balance are coming next.
{% endhint %}

Gnosis Pay issues a Visa card that spends from a smart wallet on Gnosis Chain. The card, its identity check and the wallet behind it belong to Gnosis Pay; Zold shows them alongside your euro account so that you have one place to look.

## Connect

Profile → **Card** → **Sign in with your wallet**. You sign a message with the wallet that owns your Gnosis Pay account, in your own browser wallet. Zold never sees a password or a key.

Once connected you see:

* your cards and their status,
* the balance of the card account, labelled with the time it was read,
* recent card transactions.

Every figure is a snapshot as of the moment it was fetched. Press **Refresh** for a new one.

## Signing in again

Your Gnosis Pay session is held only in your browser's memory. Reloading the page means signing in again, and the screen says so. Zold does not store it.

## What Zold does not do

Zold is not the card issuer. Cards are issued by Gnosis Pay, which runs its own verification and holds the card account. Nothing about the card is presented as a Zold card, and each card screen says whose it is.

## Coming

Card top-up from your Zold euro balance, and opening a Gnosis Pay account from inside Zold, are the next steps. Residents of the EEA, the UK and Switzerland will be eligible; regions where Gnosis Pay does not issue see the card marked unavailable while the rest of the account works as normal.
