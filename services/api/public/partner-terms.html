Partner terms

Zold is software, not a bank. Parts of your account come from partners, each under its own terms. Here is who they are, what each one does for you, and where to read their terms.

Last updated 30 Sep 2026

Monerium

Monerium ehf., Iceland. Authorised electronic money institution, supervised by the Central Bank of Iceland.

Issues your IBAN and holds your euros as e-money. Checks your identity when you connect your account on Monerium’s site. Bank transfers out of Zold are Monerium redemption orders.

You accept Monerium’s terms on Monerium’s site when you connect. Zold doesn’t send Monerium the details you type into Zold.

  • Terms for business accounts (opens in a new tab)
  • Privacy policy (opens in a new tab)

Candide

Candide Labs, France (SIREN 919 472 480).

Runs the service that sends your signed payments to the network, and covers the network fee where Zold pays it. If you choose email recovery, Candide’s recovery service sends the codes and holds your email address for it.

Zold is Candide’s customer. You don’t have an account with Candide, but its terms and privacy policy cover the data it handles for you.

  • Terms of service (opens in a new tab)
  • Privacy policy (opens in a new tab)

Circle

Circle Internet Financial Europe SAS, France, for holders in the EEA. Circle Internet Financial, LLC, USA, elsewhere.

Issues digital dollars (USDC). This only applies if someone pays you in USDC. Zold doesn’t issue or hold them; they arrive in your own account.

What this means for you

Each partner is responsible for its own service under its own terms. Zold holds no licence of its own and never holds your money. Your account is yours alone: the legal notes explain who can move your money and what happens if Zold stops operating.
