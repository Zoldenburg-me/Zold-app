Cookies and storage

What Zold stores in your browser, and why.

Last updated 30 Sep 2026

What we store

Zold uses no analytics, advertising or tracking, and loads no third-party scripts. It stores only what the service needs, so there is nothing to opt in or out of.

WhatWhy
zold_monerium_connect (cookie)Only while you connect Monerium; deleted after 10 minutes. Makes sure the sign-in finishes in the browser that started it.
Sign-in session (browser storage)Keeps you signed in until you sign out.
Device key (browser storage)Part of how this device approves payments. Don’t clear site data unless recovery is set up.
Recovery in progress (browser storage)Only while you recover an account on a new device.
Contacts and selected organisation (browser storage)Conveniences that never leave this browser.
App cache (service worker)The app’s own files, so it opens offline.
Notice dismissed (browser storage)Remembers that you closed the cookie notice.
