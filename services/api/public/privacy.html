Privacy

What personal data Zold processes, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and your rights.

Last updated 27 Oct 2026

Your passkey stays on your device. Zold receives only its public key, and no fingerprint or face data ever reaches us.

To open an account you must give a name, an email and your country; without them Zold cannot open one. Everything else is optional or comes from what you do in the app.

The blockchain is public

Your account is a smart account on the Base blockchain. Its address, its balance and every payment in or out are public and permanent there, and nobody, including Zold, can remove them. Zold does not put your name, email or IBAN on the blockchain.

How long we keep it

A sign-in session ends after 24 hours or when you sign out, and its record is deleted a day or more after that. Email verification codes expire after 15 minutes. Zold’s server does not store IP addresses.

Everything else is kept while your account exists. Zold does not yet delete account data automatically, and the app has no way to close an account yet. Write to us if you want yours closed (see your rights).

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). Where you gave consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To use any of these rights, write to support@zoldhq.com. We answer within one month. You can change your name in the app until Monerium has verified you; after that, and for a company account, write to us. Everything else, including a copy of your data, closing your account and changing your email, is done on request: the app has no button for them yet.

Payments on the public blockchain cannot be erased by anyone.

Automated decisions

At signup, Zold decides automatically whether it can open your account, from your answers alone: it refuses US persons, residents and citizens of sanctioned countries, and residents of countries it does not serve yet. If you enrol in Zoldenburg recovery, it checks automatically that the name on your 1 € payment matches the name Monerium verified, and refuses the enrolment if it does not. Nothing else about you is scored or profiled. If you think a refusal is wrong, write to us.

Complaints

You can complain to a data protection authority. The one for Zoldenburg is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, lda.bayern.de (opens in a new tab).

Cookies and storage

Zold uses no analytics, advertising or tracking, and loads no third-party scripts. It stores only what the service needs, so there is nothing to opt in or out of (§ 25 (2) TDDDG).
