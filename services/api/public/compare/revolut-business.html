Zold vs Revolut Business

Revolut Business is a bank account from Revolut Bank UAB, with deposit insurance, cards, many currencies and crypto held for you. Zold is a euro account your company holds where Zold itself can’t move the money.

Zold is in private beta and runs in test mode: no real money moves yet.

Checked against Revolut Business’s public pages on 11 Oct 2026. Their plans and prices change, so check theirs before you decide.

Which one fits

Pick Revolut Business if you want a bank with deposit insurance, many currencies and a large feature set. Pick Zold if you want an account the provider itself can’t move money from, free euro transfers, and digital dollars held in your own account.
