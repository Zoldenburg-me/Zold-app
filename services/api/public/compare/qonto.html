Zold vs Qonto

Qonto is a full business account from a French payment institution, with cards, many integrations and interest on balances. Zold is a euro account your company holds where Zold itself can’t move the money, with free transfers and a way to get paid in digital dollars.

Zold is in private beta and runs in test mode: no real money moves yet.

Checked against Qonto’s public pages on 11 Oct 2026. Their plans and prices change, so check theirs before you decide.

Which one fits

Pick Qonto if you want a mature account with cards, accounting integrations and interest on your balance. Pick Zold if clients pay you in dollars, you send many transfers, or you want an account the provider itself can’t move money from.
