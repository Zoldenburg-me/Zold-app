Zold vs Holyheld

Holyheld is an invitation-only account for global companies, with USD accounts, stablecoin ramps, invoicing and a concierge. Zold is a euro account your company holds where Zold itself can’t move the money, free to start.

Zold is in private beta and runs in test mode: no real money moves yet.

Checked against Holyheld’s public pages on 11 Oct 2026. Their plans and prices change, so check theirs before you decide.

Which one fits

Pick Holyheld if you need USD account details, a concierge and several entities under one roof. Pick Zold if you want to start free, keep euro transfers and USDC conversion free of fees, and hold an account the provider itself can’t move money from.
