Legal notes

Who provides what behind Zold, what is live today, and who can move your money. The numbers match the notes on our website.

Last updated 30 Sep 2026

1. Your euros and your IBAN

Your IBAN is issued and your euros are held as e-money (EURe) by Monerium ehf. (registered in Iceland, No. 550512-1060), authorised as an Electronic Money Institution and as a Virtual Asset Service Provider by the Financial Supervisory Authority of the Central Bank of Iceland under Act No. 17/2013 on electronic money and Act No. 18/2018 on measures against money laundering. Monerium, not Zold, checks your identity. Bank transfers out are Monerium redemption orders. Zold is software, holds no licence of its own, and is not regulated in any jurisdiction.

2. Cash pickup

Cash pickup is built and has been tested against a public test service, but no payout partner has signed. Until one does, it stays closed and the app doesn’t offer it.

3. Other countries

Today money leaves by bank transfer (SEPA) to European IBANs. Payouts to other countries depend on partners we’re talking to but haven’t signed. The app only lists destinations it can actually price and pay.

4. Who can move your money

Your account is a Safe smart account on the Base network, and the passkey on your device is its only owner. Every payment is signed by that passkey for one amount to one recipient. Zold’s server prepares the payment but holds no key that can move or block your money. Monerium, as issuer, can freeze e-money under its own legal obligations. Recovery is optional and chosen during setup. If you name Zoldenburg as your recovery guardian, Zoldenburg can start moving your account to a new passkey after verifying your identity against Monerium’s records. The move completes only after a waiting period, during which your current device can cancel it. If you choose no recovery and lose your device, the account can’t be recovered, and only euros can be reclaimed from Monerium. If Zold stops operating, the same applies: your euros remain reclaimable from Monerium.

5. Business accounts

Team members, roles, payment approvals, invoices, the address book, the chart of accounts and CSV export work today. Only the euro account is open. USD and GBP wait on an account provider, and KES and INR on a local payout partner. The app says which, rather than looking as if they work. A company does not yet get its own payment account, so a business account is funded from a member’s personal account, and the app tells you before you do this. There are no cards for business yet. The paid plan cannot be charged yet. Each organisation can start one free 30-day trial of it. Downgrading pauses paid features and deletes nothing.

Early software

Zold is early and still changing. The screens on our website are illustrations, not real balances. Nothing there is financial advice or an offer of a regulated payment service. Money held on chain is not covered by any deposit insurance scheme.
