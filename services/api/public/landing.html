PROTOTYPE STATUS — CAPITAL AT RISK

Zold is a prototype running on sandbox e-money and local test rails where noted below. Availability, custody and payout behaviour are under active development, balances shown in this page's screens are illustrative, and nothing here is an offer of a regulated payment service or financial advice.

FOR EURO ACCOUNTS AND BANK PAYOUTS

1 Your IBAN is issued and your euros are held as e-money (EURe) by Monerium ehf. (registered in Iceland No. 550512-1060), authorised as an Electronic Money Institution and as a Virtual Asset Service Provider by the Financial Supervisory Authority of the Central Bank of Iceland under Act No. 17/2013 governing the issuance of electronic money and Act No. 18/2018 governing measures for countering money laundering. SEPA payouts are executed as Monerium redemption orders. Zold operates against Monerium's sandbox environment; it is software, holds no licence of its own, and is not separately regulated in any jurisdiction.

FOR CASH PICKUP

2 Cash pickup is implemented against the SEP-10 / SEP-24 anchor protocol and verified against a public test anchor. No commercial payout agreement is in place; references returned in sandbox mode are generated, and production availability depends on a licensed partner. Cash pickup is a money transmission, so sender identity data is provided to the anchor before a withdrawal opens, and a payout is refused rather than opened when required details are missing. FX rates are indicative and refreshed from a rate endpoint where available; a quote locks a rate for ten minutes and expires rather than repricing silently.

FOR LOCAL SPEND THROUGH NOAH

3 Noah integration is the planned local-spend expansion layer. Noah's public docs describe crypto-to-fiat payouts across 60+ countries, 120+ fiat currencies, and local payment methods including bank transfer, real-time payment systems such as PIX, UPI and SPEI, mobile money, digital wallets and cash pickup. Production availability depends on Noah approval, live channel queries, per-country dynamic forms, limits, KYC/KYB reliance or hosted onboarding, and corridor-specific legal review.

FOR CUSTODY AND APPROVALS

4 Each account is a Safe smart account; deployment gas is sponsored. A passkey is registered at onboarding and every payout is signed against that exact amount and recipient. Today the account's owner key is still held server-side, so the account is custodial in practice — replacing that key with passkey and co-signer approval is in progress. Assets held on chain are not covered by any government or private deposit insurance scheme.

FOR BUSINESS ACCOUNTS

5 Members, roles, payment approvals, invoices, the address book, the chart of accounts and CSV export are implemented. Accounts are modelled in several currencies, but only the euro account is open: USD and GBP need an account provider that has not been granted, and KES and INR need a local payout partner that is not contracted. Each currency states in the app what it is waiting on rather than appearing as though it works. An organisation does not yet get a payment account of its own — until it does, a business account is funded from a member's personal account, which the app says explicitly before you do it. Cards are not built. Free and paid plans are described in-app; the paid plan is not yet chargeable and the 30-day trial is granted on request. Downgrading pauses paid features and deletes nothing.
