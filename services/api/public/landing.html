    Questions

    Is Zold a bank?

    No. Zold is an app. Your IBAN and your euros come from Monerium ehf., an electronic money institution licensed in Iceland and supervised by the Central Bank of Iceland. Under the EU E-Money Directive (2009/110/EC), that licence is passported across the European Economic Area, so Monerium can serve you anywhere in the EEA. Zold holds no licence of its own: Monerium looks after the regulated money, and we focus on what we do best, building the software you use it with.

    Do I have to leave my bank?

    No. Many people start by having their salary or client payments land in Zold. Send money to your other bank any time.

    What does it cost?

    Opening an account is free, with no monthly fee. Sending euros anywhere in Europe is free.

    How does the card work?

    It spends straight from your euro balance. Use it virtually right away, add it to Apple Pay or Google Pay, or order a physical card.

    Can people abroad pay me?

    Yes. By euro bank transfer from anywhere in Europe, or in USDC (digital dollars) from outside Europe, through your link.

    What if I lose my phone?

    With recovery on, you sign in on your new phone, confirm it’s you, and your account moves over after a short wait.

    My employer only takes German IBANs.

    In the euro area, an employer can’t refuse an IBAN because of the country it’s from. Your Zold IBAN works for salary like any other.

    How is my money protected?

    Your euros are e-money, backed one-for-one and held in an account in your name. E-money isn’t covered by deposit guarantee schemes.

    Who can move my money?

    Only you. Every payment needs your approval with the passkey on your device. Zold holds no key to your account, so it can’t move your money. Recovery is your choice: if you pick Zoldenburg as your recovery guardian, it can hand your account to your new phone after verifying it’s you, but never move money, and you can cancel the handover from your old phone during the waiting period. Add your own wallet as a second key instead, and Zold has no part in it at all. The one other party is Monerium: as the issuer of your euros, it can freeze them where the law requires it.

    Who is behind Zold?

    Zold is made by Zoldenburg. Your IBAN and e-money are provided by Monerium ehf., a licensed e-money institution supervised by the Central Bank of Iceland.
