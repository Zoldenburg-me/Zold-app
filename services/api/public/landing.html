Questions

Is Zold a bank?

No. Zold is an app. Your IBAN and your euros come from Monerium ehf., a licensed e-money institution in Iceland, supervised by the Central Bank of Iceland. Zold holds no licence of its own.

Is my money insured like in a bank?

No. Euros held as e-money aren’t covered by a deposit insurance scheme. Monerium must keep them backed one-for-one, and they sit in your own account, not pooled with Zold’s money.

Who can move my money?

Only you. Every payment needs your approval with the passkey on your device, the same kind of sign-in as Face ID or Touch ID. Zold’s servers can prepare a payment but hold no key that can send it. Monerium, as the issuer of your euros, can freeze them where the law requires it. If you turn on recovery with Zoldenburg, Zoldenburg can start moving your account to a new device after checking your identity. There’s a waiting period, and you can cancel it from your old device.

What if I lose my phone?

If you set up recovery, you set up Face ID or fingerprint sign-in on a new phone and confirm it’s you. After a waiting period, your account moves to the new device. If you skipped recovery, your account can’t be recovered, but your euros can still be reclaimed from Monerium.

What does it cost?

Opening an account is free, there’s no monthly fee, and Zold charges nothing to send euros to a bank account in Europe.

Do I need to know anything about crypto?

No. You see euros, an IBAN and bank transfers. If someone wants to pay you in USDC, a digital dollar, you can accept it too, but you never have to.

Why doesn’t my IBAN start with DE?

Your IBAN is issued by Monerium. It works for euro bank transfers across Europe like any other euro IBAN.
