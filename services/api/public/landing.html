FOR BUSINESS

Your company's money, with the controls a company needs.

An organisation holds the accounts, not a person. Invite your team, give each of them only what their job needs, and put a second pair of eyes in front of every payment that leaves.5

Roles that separate the books from the money
Your bookkeeper can categorise every transaction and never send a euro. Viewer, accountant, payer, admin, owner.
Four eyes on every payment run
Whoever drafts a payment cannot approve it. And if a payee's bank details change after approval, the payment stops instead of following them.
Invoices your suppliers can actually use
Send a one-time link. They fill in the invoice with no account, no wallet and no onboarding, and you pay it from the same screen.
Books your accountant recognises
Your own chart of accounts, rules that map transactions to it automatically, cost basis, and a CSV that opens in the ledger you already keep.

EARLY SOFTWARE

Zold is early and still changing. The screens on this page are illustrations, not real balances. Nothing here is financial advice or an offer of a regulated payment service. Money held on chain is not covered by any deposit insurance scheme.

YOUR EUROS AND YOUR IBAN

1 Your IBAN is issued and your euros are held as e-money (EURe) by Monerium ehf. (registered in Iceland No. 550512-1060), authorised as an Electronic Money Institution and as a Virtual Asset Service Provider by the Financial Supervisory Authority of the Central Bank of Iceland under Act No. 17/2013 governing the issuance of electronic money and Act No. 18/2018 governing measures for countering money laundering. Your identity check is done by Monerium, not by Zold. SEPA payouts are Monerium redemption orders. Zold is software, holds no licence of its own, and is not regulated in any jurisdiction.

CASH PICKUP

2 Cash pickup is built and has been tested against a public test service, but no payout partner is signed. Until one is, it stays closed and the app does not offer it.

OTHER COUNTRIES

3 Today money leaves by SEPA to European IBANs. Payouts to other countries depend on partners we are talking to but have not signed. The app only lists destinations it can actually price and pay.

WHO CAN MOVE YOUR MONEY

4 Your account is a Safe smart account, and the passkey on your device is its owner. Every payment is signed by that passkey for one amount to one recipient. Zold's server prepares the payment and pays the network fee, but holds no key that can move your money. If you lose the device, recovery by email, where offered, moves the account to a new passkey after a waiting period you can cancel.

BUSINESS ACCOUNTS

5 Team members, roles, payment approvals, invoices, the address book, the chart of accounts and CSV export work today. Only the euro account is open. USD and GBP wait on an account provider, and KES and INR on a local payout partner; the app says which, rather than looking as if they work. A company does not yet get its own payment account, so a business account is funded from a member's personal account, and the app tells you before you do it. There are no cards for business yet. The paid plan cannot be charged yet; the 30-day trial is given on request. Downgrading pauses paid features and deletes nothing.
