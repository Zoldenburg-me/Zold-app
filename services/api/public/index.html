Only this device can spend it.

Your passkey is the account's signature. It is created here, it stays here, and it is what every payment is checked against.
🔑 Nothing to remember Face ID, your fingerprint or your screen lock. No password, no seed phrase, nothing to write down.
✍️ You sign the amount and the recipient Both are fixed before you approve, so a payment cannot be re-priced or redirected after the fact.
🚫 We cannot move your balance Our server pays the network fee and submits what you signed. It cannot authorise a payment of its own.
Keep this device. A passkey lives on the device that created it, so setting one up on your everyday phone or laptop is the right choice.

A lost phone is not a lost account.

Recovery is a guardian on your smart wallet, run by Candide, that can move the account to a new passkey only after a code on every registered channel and a waiting period.
✉️ A code, then a wait Whoever starts a recovery must pass a code on every channel you registered. Then the on-chain waiting period runs before anything changes.
✋ You can stop it While the wait runs, this device can cancel the recovery with one passkey approval. It shows on your profile.
🚫 Zold cannot do it alone The guardian key is Candide's, the waiting period is enforced on chain, and Zold holds no key that can move your account.
Someone who controls your mailbox can start a recovery. The waiting period is what lets you stop one, so keep this device and look at your profile if anything seems off.
